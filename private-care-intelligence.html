Private Care Intelligence

Why Private

Every family we work with is carrying something they can't post about, hand off to a call center, or solve with a generic checklist. Private Care Intelligence is a confidential, one-on-one engagement for families facing the hardest decisions of their parents' lives — where they should live, how to talk to siblings who don't agree, what to do about the cognitive decline no one else has named out loud. You get direct access to me, not a rotating team of case managers. I map your family's exact situation using the same frameworks I bring to C-Suite leaders and university presidents, then hand you marching orders instead of more homework. Whether you're the one physically in the room or you're quarterbacking care decisions from another state, this is built to prevent the irreversible mistakes, protect your parent's dignity, and keep your family from colossal clashes when the stakes are highest. Discretion isn't a feature here — it's the foundation. Nothing about your family's situation is shared, studied, or used as a case example without your explicit permission.

How It Works

One Family. One Strategy. Complete Discretion.

Private Care Intelligence begins with a confidential conversation — not a sales call, not an intake form read by someone you'll never speak to again. You talk directly with Tiffany about where your family stands and what's most urgent right now.

From there, I map the full picture using the Golden Lens™ Approach: your parent's care needs, the family dynamics no one wants to say out loud, and the decision points already headed your way. Nothing is templated. Nothing is outsourced.

You leave with a guided action plan, in order, so this never becomes a rushed solo-mission — whether you're the one physically doing the caregiving or coordinating decisions from another state.

Engagements continue for as long as your family needs them — through appointments, transitions, and the curveballs no one can plan for in advance.

Every conversation stays exactly where it happens.Your family's situation is never used as a public case study, shared with other clients, or referenced without your explicit permission.
